MODERNARTEHUB

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ModernArteHub handles data in the ModernArte app. ModernArte is a private tool used only by the owner of the ModernArteHub Etsy shop to prepare and publish Pins that promote the shop's own printable art on the owner's Pinterest account.

Data we access

With the owner's permission, granted through Pinterest's OAuth login, the app accesses the connected Pinterest account's basic profile, boards and Pins, and creates Pins and boards on that account. It does not access data of any other Pinterest user.

How data is used

Pinterest data is used only to list the owner's boards, create Pins that link to the shop's Etsy listings, and show the status of published Pins. Every Pin is reviewed and approved by the owner before it is published.

Storage

Access tokens and app data are stored encrypted on the owner's personal computer. Nothing is stored on external servers.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share Pinterest data with anyone. Listing images and texts may be processed by OpenAI to write Pin titles and descriptions. No Pinterest user data is sent to OpenAI.

Data deletion

The owner can disconnect the app at any time from Pinterest settings, which revokes access. All locally stored tokens are then deleted.

Shop customers

Purchases are handled entirely by Etsy. Customer data from orders is governed by Etsy's Privacy Policy and is not used by the ModernArte app.

Contact

For any question about this policy, write to fontanaalessio03@gmail.com.
